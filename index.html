Step 1

Arrive

Ears in the wind, nerves at the door.

Check in at the desk and settle into a calm waiting area. We'll call you as soon as we're ready.

Step 2

Meet

Hello first. Exam second.

Treats, a slow introduction and a chance to sniff the room before anything starts.

Step 3

Check

A careful, nose-to-tail look.

We explain what we're checking as we go, so you're never left guessing.

Step 4

Care

Gentle hands, clear plans.

Any treatment is talked through with you first — options, aftercare and cost.

Step 5

Recover

Rest, snuggles, the cone of shame.

You'll head home with simple written instructions and someone to call with questions.

Step 6

Go home

Back to the zoomies.

We'll remind you when the next check is due. Until then — go be best friends.

Our approach

We believe a vet visit should feel less like a hospital and more like visiting a friend who happens to be very, very good with stethoscopes.

naps are medicine ✦

Four things we never skip.

  1. 01

    Calm before clinical

    Slow introductions, treats and patience come first. A relaxed pet gets a better exam.

  2. 02

    Explained, not decided for you

    We walk you through findings, options and costs so every decision is made together.

  3. 03

    Prevention over cure

    Regular checkups, vaccines and dental care keep more pets out of the emergency room.

  4. 04

    A relationship, not a visit

    We want to know your pet over a lifetime: puppy zoomies, adult adventures, senior naps.
